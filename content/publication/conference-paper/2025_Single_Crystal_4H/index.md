abstract: |
  4H-SiC devices are usually considered uncompetitive for low-voltage applications due to substrate resistance limitations. This work challenges this long-held belief by demonstrating a novel 150 mm single-crystal 4H/3C-SiC engineered substrate with resistivity over 45× lower than conventional 4H-SiC. The engineered substrate leverages the ultra-low resistivity of 3C-SiC and overcomes its high defect density by integrating a ~580 nm prime-grade 4H-SiC template. This template is transferred via surface-activated bonding and ion-cut onto a liquid-phase-grown 3C-SiC substrate.
