abstract: "GaN multi-channel material has recently emerged as a promising platform for RF and power devices due to its high current capacity and low sheet resistance. Here we demonstrate, for the first time, a multi-channel GaN monolithic bidirectional switch (MBDS), achieving symmetric breakdown voltage (BV) up to 5 kV, specific on-resistance of 20 mΩcm2, and threshold voltage of 0.8 V in both voltage/current polarities. This BV is the highest reported in MBDS devices to date. This device is fabricated on a 150-mm 5-channel GaN-on-sapphire wafer and features a dual junction termination extension for electric field management. Enhancement-mode (E-mode) is achieved by a monolithic dual-cascode architecture, which integrates two low-voltage, single-channel E-mode HEMTs on either side of the multi-channel region.
