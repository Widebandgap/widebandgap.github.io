abstract: |
  The rapid advancement of WBG and UWBG power semiconductors has propelled modern power electronics toward high switching frequencies and power densities. However, these high-stress operating regimes introduce severe EMI and isolation challenges. To overcome these bottlenecks, the integration of photonic technologies into power electronic systems has emerged as a promising solution. This article presents a comprehensive review and perspective on the state-of-the-art in optical power electronics.
